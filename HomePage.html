OUR ECO-FRIENDLY MISSION

OUR MISSION IS TO INSPIRE ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY THROUGH VARIOUS ACTIVITIES LIKE ENGAGING IN NATURE WALKS, INTERACTIVE ONLINE AND OFFLINE AWARENESS SESSIONS, AND FUN ENVIRONMENT QUIZZES. WE ENCOURAGE CRITICAL THINKING BY COMMISSIONING CLIMATE AND ENVIRONMENTAL ARTICLE WRITING TO AMPLIFY YOUTH VOICES. THROUGH CLOTHES DONATION DRIVES, WE PROMOTE SUSTAINABILITY AND REDUCE WASTE. WE FOCUS ON YOUTH EDUCATION BY ORGANIZING HANDS-ON SEMINARS ON VERMICOMPOSTING AND ECO-FRIENDLY PRACTICES. TOGETHER, WE STRIVE TO BUILD A GREENER, CONSCIOUS AND RESPONSIBLE FUTURE
